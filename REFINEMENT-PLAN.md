## Work plan

### Phase 1 — Trust and content accuracy
- Replace the placeholder TURQUOISE Facebook destination with the verified official page URL once supplied.
- Verify the “07+ years” and “100+ projects” claims; revise or remove any figure that cannot be substantiated.
- Replace generic “Skill Certificate 01–06” labels with exact certificate names, issuers and dates.

### Phase 2 — Bold visual system
- Preserve the existing dark / turquoise identity and recognizable hero.
- Strengthen editorial typography, scale contrast, section transitions and project art direction.
- Use purposeful motion (scroll reveals, hover feedback and project transitions), not motion everywhere.
- Keep sound opt-in and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Refine the hero to communicate business + creativity + technology in one concise statement.

### Phase 3 — Curated work and case studies
- Lead with 8–12 strongest projects instead of giving every item equal prominence.
- Group the archive into Brand & Identity, Campaigns, Social Impact, Visual Communication and Motion.
- Create case-study pages or modal details with brief, role, deliverables, tools and outcome.
- Distinguish personal/concept work from commissioned or organizational work.
- Remove duplicate or near-duplicate entries where they do not add a distinct story.

### Phase 4 — Navigation and conversion
- Simplify primary navigation to About, Work, Experience, Journey and Contact.
- Move secondary sections into a compact menu or in-page navigation.
- Make CV access clear without competing with the main contact action.
- Give Contact a clear collaboration / opportunity call to action.

### Phase 5 — Performance, accessibility and SEO
- Generate optimized responsive thumbnails (WebP/AVIF) and lazy-load non-critical media.
- Defer full-resolution images and video until requested.
- Check keyboard navigation, focus visibility, contrast, image alt text and dialog focus handling.
- Validate mobile layouts at narrow phone, tablet and desktop widths.
- Verify page title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph image, favicon and sitemap.
